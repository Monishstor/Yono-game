## 2024-05-24 - Accessibility improvements on PromoCodeVault\n**Learning:** Missing aria-labels on icon-only buttons and inputs in modals is a common accessibility trap. Empty form submissions without disabled buttons can also lead to bad UX.\n**Action:** Add aria-labels to icon buttons/inputs and use disabled states (with proper styling like `opacity-50` and `cursor-not-allowed`) for dependent submit buttons across modals.
